    How to Play

    You command the protagonists (white). The AI commands the youkai bosses (black).

    • Standard chess movement. No check, no checkmate, no stalemate.
    • The game ends only when a king is captured.
    • Any capture triggers a danmaku boss fight: survive the boss and the capture goes through; lose and your piece is the one captured.
    • Castle "en passant": if you castle through a square an enemy piece attacks, that piece can capture your king the following turn on the square your king is now on.
    • No draws — the game continues until a king falls.
