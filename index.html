      How to Play

      You command the protagonists (white). The AI commands the youkai bosses (black).

      • Standard chess movement, including castling, en passant and promotion (a pawn reaching the back rank becomes a queen, rook, bishop or knight of your choice). No check, no checkmate, no stalemate.
      • The game ends only when a king is captured.
      • Any capture triggers a danmaku boss fight: win it and the attacking piece is removed — your capture goes through, or the bosses' attack is blocked; lose it and the piece you moved is captured instead. Your captures are Normal fights; the bosses' are Lunatic.
      • Castle "en passant": if you castle through a square an enemy piece attacks, or your king was already in check on its starting square, the enemy gains a one-turn right: any of their pieces attacking that middle square may capture whatever is on the square your king landed on (usually the king itself).
      • No draws — the game continues until a king falls.
      • Boss fights: arrows/WASD to move, Space or X to bomb, hold Shift to focus (slower movement, smaller hitbox). Your firepower scales with the piece's value — a Cirno pawn fires weakly, a Reimu king rips through cards.
      • Practice Mode: the "Practice" button opens a menu to fight any boss as any piece on either difficulty — no board, no stakes. Great for learning a character.

      Multiplayer

      Play a friend over the internet (peer-to-peer). Host a room and share the code, or join one you were given. You’ll be White if you host, Black if you join.
